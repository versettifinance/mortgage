For over fifteen years, our team has helped thousands of Ontario families move from renting to owning — funding more than a billion dollars in mortgages along the way. But the number we're proudest of isn't the dollars. It's the number of clients who come back for their next home, and send their friends and family to us.

You're never handed off to a call centre or left chasing one busy person. Every client is supported by a dedicated team — the way a major bank operates — but with the personal attention, flexibility, and lender choice that only an independent brokerage can offer. Whoever you speak to has your full file and your full history in front of them.

We're a mortgage brokerage, which means we don't work for a bank — we work for you. We shop your file across hundreds of lenders: the big banks, credit unions, monoline lenders, and where the situation calls for it, select international lenders. Then we negotiate on your behalf. Lenders compete; you get the option that genuinely fits your situation, not just the one product a single bank happens to sell.

The Team Behind Your Mortgage

  • Mortgage advisors who understand your goals and map out your options across our full lender network.
  • Underwriting specialists who structure and package your file to give it the strongest possible chance of approval at the best rate.
  • Closing coordinators who work directly with your lawyer and lender so possession day goes smoothly.
  • A dedicated point of contact for you throughout — backed by the whole team, so you're never waiting on one person's schedule.

What "White-Glove, End-to-End" Actually Means

Most brokers show up once you're ready to sign and disappear the moment your mortgage funds. We do the opposite — we're with you before, during, and long after:

  • Before you're even ready: we'll review your finances, find hidden savings leakage, and build a personalized plan to get you mortgage-ready faster — at no cost and no obligation.
  • Through your purchase: pre-approval strategy across our full lender network, lender negotiation, and a complete, no-surprises breakdown of every cost before you commit.
  • On closing day: coordination with your lawyer and lender so the keys land in your hand without a hitch.
  • Long after you've moved in: annual mortgage check-ups, prepayment strategies to pay your home off years faster, renewal negotiation so you never auto-sign a bad rate, and equity planning for your next move.

And here's what we won't do: we won't ask for your Social Insurance Number, pull your credit, or request sensitive documents until we've actually spoken and you've decided you want to work with us. Everything up to that point — every calculator, every plan, every conversation — is free, private, and no-obligation.

You only ever "pay" us by trusting us with your mortgage when you're ready. That's the entire deal. No hidden fees, no pressure, no catch.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this really free? What's the catch?
Yes — every review, plan, and consultation is free. Like all mortgage brokers in Ontario, we're paid a commission by the lender when your mortgage closes, not by you. If you never proceed, you owe nothing.
Will you pull my credit? Does it hurt my score?
Never without your explicit consent. When you're ready for a pre-approval, we do one soft-touch process across our lender network.
Are these calculators accurate?
They use real Canadian rules: semi-annual compounding, the federal OSFI stress test, current CMHC premium tiers, and 2026 Ontario and Toronto land transfer tax brackets. They're estimates for planning — final numbers come from a lender-verified pre-approval, free with us.
Do you only work with one bank?
No — we shop your file across hundreds of lenders: major banks, credit unions, monoline lenders, and where the situation calls for it, select international lenders. Then we negotiate. Lenders compete; you win.
I'm self-employed. Can you help?
Yes — incorporated professionals and self-employed buyers are a specialty. Lenders assess your income differently; we know which ones look at gross-ups, retained earnings, and stated income programs.

Your Privacy

Privacy & Confidentiality

Your financial information is deeply personal. Here's exactly how we protect it.

At Get Me Mortgage, protecting your privacy isn't a formality — it's fundamental to the trust our business is built on. We handle every piece of your information in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and all applicable provincial privacy legislation.

What We Collect — and When

You can use every calculator, guide, and planning tool on this website completely anonymously. We only collect personal information when you choose to share it — by submitting the contact form, requesting our checklist, or speaking with us directly.

We do not ask for your Social Insurance Number, credit authorization, or sensitive financial documents until we have spoken with you and you have decided to move forward. Nothing before that point requires this information.

How We Use Your Information

Any information you provide is used solely to help you with your mortgage or home-buying needs — to answer your questions, prepare your options, and, if you choose to proceed, to arrange your mortgage with a lender on your behalf.

Confidentiality & Third Parties

Everything you discuss with us remains strictly confidential and private. We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party without your prior, explicit consent. When you decide to proceed with a mortgage application, we share only the information necessary with the specific lender(s) you have authorized us to approach — and nothing more.

Document Handling & Retention

We retain your documents only as long as necessary to serve you and to meet our legal and regulatory obligations. Once your mortgage process is complete and any required retention period has passed, all physical documents are securely shredded and all digital records are permanently and securely deleted, in line with industry best practices for the safe disposal of sensitive financial information.

Data Security

We use reasonable physical, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss. Information submitted through this website is transmitted over a secure, encrypted connection.

Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to request corrections, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, or if you have any questions about how your information is handled, contact us at versettifinance@gmail.com or +1 (647) 952-2740.
